Questions people actually ask

The short answers.

How does it read my transactions?

With your permission, the app reads the notifications your banking and UPI apps post to your phone and picks out the amount and merchant. It does not read your SMS, and it never connects to your bank account. This feature is available in India today; elsewhere it is marked "coming soon" and you add expenses manually.

Where is my data stored?

Your personal transactions are kept in local storage on your phone. Your account details and any splits you share with other people are stored on our servers so the other people in the split can see them. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Do I have to pay?

No. The free tier tracks unlimited personal expenses. Premium removes ads and lifts the limits on splits, analytics and statement import.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open the Play Store app → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Daily Expense Tracker → Cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for.

Which countries is the app available in?

Over 100 countries, with prices set in the local currency. Automatic notification tracking and receipt scanning are currently India-only; everything else works everywhere.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not today. The app is Android only.
