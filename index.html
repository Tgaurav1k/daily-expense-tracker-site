Questions

The things people actually ask.

How does it read my transactions?

With your permission it reads the notifications your banking and UPI apps post to your phone, and pulls out the amount and merchant. It does not read your SMS and it never connects to your bank account. This works in India today; elsewhere it is marked "coming soon" and you add expenses yourself.

Where does my data live?

Your personal transactions stay in local storage on your phone. Your account details and any split you share go to our servers, because the other people in the split need to see them. The detail is in the privacy policy.

Do I have to pay?

No. The free tier tracks unlimited personal expenses. Premium removes ads and lifts the limits on splits, analytics and statement import.

How do I cancel Premium?

Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Daily Expense Tracker → Cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for.

Which countries can use it?

Over 100, with prices in the local currency. Automatic notification tracking and receipt scanning are India-only right now; everything else works everywhere.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Android only.
