Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Daily Expense Tracker is free to download and use. The only paid item is Premium, an auto-renewing monthly subscription.

All payments go through Google Play

We never take your card details and we do not process payments ourselves. Premium is sold through Google Play billing, so Google is the seller of record and Google's payment and refund terms apply to every purchase. That also means cancellations and refunds are requested through Google Play, not through us.

Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel at any time. There is no cancellation fee and no minimum term.

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right).
  3. Go to Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  4. Select Daily Expense Tracker, then tap Cancel subscription.

You can also open the app and go to Premium → Manage subscription, which takes you to the same place.

After cancelling you keep Premium until the end of the period you have already paid for. It then stops renewing and your account returns to the free tier. Nothing you have recorded is deleted when Premium ends.

Refunds

Because Google is the seller of record, refunds are issued by Google under the Google Play refund policy. In practice:

  • Google offers a self-service refund window for recent purchases directly in the Play Store. If you are inside that window, requesting it there is the fastest route.
  • Outside that window you can still submit a refund request to Google, who decide the outcome.
  • We do not charge a cancellation fee and we do not withhold a refund that Google approves.

Partial refunds for the unused part of a billing period are not issued automatically, since cancelling already lets you use the time you paid for.

Something went wrong with a payment?

If you were charged but Premium did not activate, or you were charged after cancelling, email automationbuddy1k@gmail.com with the Google Play order number from your receipt. We will look into it and, where the fault is ours, support your refund request with Google directly. We aim to reply within two business days.

Free trials and promotional codes

If a free trial or promotional code is offered, the terms shown at the time of the offer apply. A promotional code that unlocks Premium at no charge is not a purchase and carries no refund.

Price changes

Prices are shown in your local currency in the app before you subscribe. If the price of an existing subscription changes, Google notifies you in advance and asks you to accept the new price before it is charged. You can cancel instead.
